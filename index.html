About Operlark

Technology updates with a useful next step.

Operlark is an independent, single-owner digital media project for people exploring better ways to work with software. We cover AI tools, app integrations, automation and SaaS updates.

Grounded editorial work

Public announcements and original source material guide our coverage. AI assists drafting; source evidence and editorial checks remain part of the workflow. Posts are skipped when suitable fresh news is unavailable.

Original visual explanations

We prepare our own typography and explanatory graphics for visual platforms. We link readers to the source so they can check availability and details.

Transparent monetization

Relevant posts may include the registered Make referral link with an advertising disclosure. An affiliate relationship does not mean a service is necessary, suitable for everyone or guaranteed to produce results.

Publishing & application

An owner-operated media workflow.

Operlark uses GitHub Actions for scheduled editorial processing, Groq for AI-assisted text drafting and Buffer for connected social publishing. Metricool is connected to the Operlark Pinterest account. Google Drive is used for operational records.

X and Threads have live publishing results. Instagram also has a live news publishing result; independent owner-page verification is recorded separately from logged-out visibility. A separate official Pinterest API integration is being prepared for the owner’s existing account; its registered app is awaiting Trial review, and OAuth and Standard production access remain pending. These information pages do not connect visitors’ social accounts or offer a customer login service.

Operlark is independent of Pinterest and the other platforms mentioned here. Their names identify the services we use; no partnership, certification or API approval is implied.
