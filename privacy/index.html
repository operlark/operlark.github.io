Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026
Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy describes information handled by Operlark’s official information pages and its owner-operated news media workflow. Operlark is an independent, single-owner digital media project. For privacy questions, contact operlark@gmail.com.

Current scope

These pages are informational. They have no account registration, contact form, checkout or visitor social-account connection. The official Pinterest API integration is being prepared; this policy does not claim that live OAuth authorization or production API access has already been approved.

1. Information handled

  • Website requests. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP address, requested URL, time of access and browser information, to deliver and protect the site. Operlark has not added its own visitor analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Correspondence. If you email Operlark, the email address, name, message and any attachments you choose to send are handled through Gmail. Please do not send passwords, API keys, authentication codes or sensitive financial information.
  • Editorial material. The media workflow retrieves public official announcements, publication dates, article URLs and excerpts. It generates drafts, source evidence, captions and original images.
  • Operational records. Records include Operlark’s own connected-account identifiers, source links, post identifiers and URLs, publishing status, error classifications and available aggregate post metrics. These records support publishing checks, duplicate prevention and analysis.
  • Affiliate records. The approved Make referral link is recorded separately from any reported clicks, conversions, commission and payouts. Unavailable figures are recorded as unavailable rather than as zero. Confirmed conversion, commission and payout data have not yet been retrieved by the automated workflow.

2. How information is used

Information is used to deliver these pages, respond to correspondence, prepare source-grounded editorial content, publish to the owner’s connected accounts, verify delivery, prevent duplicate posts, investigate errors and assess available performance. The current workflow does not collect audience passwords, private messages, individual follower profiles or lists of commenters for profiling or targeted advertising.

3. Services used

  • GitHub Pages: hosting these public information pages in a separate public website repository.
  • OpenAI / ChatGPT Work: used by the operator for development and operational work.
  • GitHub and GitHub Actions: private automation source, publishing records and scheduled processing. A separate public media repository stores only publishable original image files.
  • Groq: AI-assisted drafting from selected public article material and editorial instructions. Social-account credentials and contact correspondence are not inputs to the current editorial generation prompt.
  • Buffer: publishing and reading delivery status and available post metrics for the owner’s connected X, Threads and Instagram accounts.
  • Metricool: the existing Operlark Pinterest connection and supported operational or analytics access.
  • Google Drive, Google Sheets and Gmail: operational records and correspondence. Unattended synchronization from GitHub Actions to Google Sheets is awaiting a separate owner OAuth authorization.
  • Make: the approved affiliate destination. Make and its affiliate systems may process referral visits and any subsequent registration or purchase under their own policies.
  • Social platforms: X, Threads, Instagram and Pinterest process activity on their services under their own policies.

These providers may process information outside your country. Their storage, security and retention practices are governed by their own terms and policies. Operlark does not control their systems.

4. Pinterest API connection

The prepared integration is intended for the owner’s existing operlark account, rather than for connecting accounts of website visitors. It is intended to use the official OAuth flow and the limited scopes boards:read, pins:read, pins:write and user_accounts:read. It would read the authorized account and board to check the publishing destination, submit the owner’s original Pin content and read back the Pin to check delivery.

Live API authorization and production access remain pending. No live OAuth data has been collected through this prepared connection. We do not claim Standard access or a Pinterest partnership. Activation remains subject to Pinterest’s review, terms and permitted data handling. Pinterest account credentials or session cookies are not collected by the application. Pinterest API information is not used as input to the current AI drafting prompt, to train models, to profile other accounts or to build advertising audiences.

5. Disclosure and public content

Original images, captions, posts and their source links are made public when published. Private automation files, credentials and financial management records are not intended for publication. The current workflow does not sell personal information or share Pinterest API account information with affiliate advertisers. Relevant affiliate posts disclose the referral relationship; visiting a referral link takes you to a separate service.

6. Cookies and external links

Operlark has not added cookies, local storage, tracking pixels, embedded social feeds or third-party analytics scripts to these pages. The hosting provider may use its own technical or security mechanisms. Social and affiliate services may use cookies or tracking after you visit them. Ordinary links do not embed those services into these pages.

7. Storage, retention and security

Active API credentials for the automation are stored in GitHub Actions Secrets, rather than in the public pages or public image files. Operational records are kept in the private automation repository and the Operlark management spreadsheet. Generated publication images are stored publicly. Current workflow artifacts are configured for seven-day retention, but private repository history, management records, email and provider backups may remain longer.

The prepared Pinterest token-refresh code includes encrypted storage for rotated refresh-token state in the private repository; this connection is not yet live, and this statement is not a claim that Pinterest data has already been processed or encrypted. The current implementation has no automated deletion schedule for all operational records and no self-service deletion portal. Retention depends on the record’s operational purpose and the provider involved. No claim of a formal security certification, independent audit or guaranteed breach prevention is made.

8. Requests and control

Email operlark@gmail.com to ask about information associated with your correspondence, request a correction or request deletion of information under Operlark’s control. The operator reviews requests manually and may need to verify the request. Do not provide a password or authentication code as verification. Provider retention, public copies and records outside Operlark’s control may limit what can be removed.

Social-platform privacy settings and deletion tools control information held on those platforms. An account owner can revoke an authorized application through the platform’s settings. Operlark does not claim that revocation automatically removes every historical operational record.

9. Children

These pages and the owner-operated publishing application are intended for a general adult professional audience, not for children under 13. Operlark does not run a child-directed registration or data-collection service. Contact the operator if you believe a child has provided personal information by email.

10. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the implementation or data processing changes. The date above identifies the latest version. Privacy and editorial contact: operlark@gmail.com.
